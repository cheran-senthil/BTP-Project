\chapter*{Note on the revised edition}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Note on the revised edition}
The April 2018 report surveyed partition rank inequalities and cubic
partition-pair congruences. It reproduced a partial proof of Mao's
$M_2$-rank inequality and described an unsuccessful attempt to extend it to
the progression $9m+8$.

This edition adds a complete elementary proof in
Section~\ref{sec:completion}, including an explicit lower bound. Its inputs
are the generating-function and theta-function identities already used in
the original report. No claim of priority is made for the elementary
argument or its lower bound.

All six chapters have been edited for mathematical notation, exposition,
cross-references, and bibliographic accuracy. The discussion of cubic
partition pairs remains an exposition of previously published work.
Chapter~\ref{ch:lin} also records Chern's earlier resolution of Lin's
conjectures.

The original report, including its title page and supervisor's certificate,
is preserved without alteration as \texttt{report-2018.pdf}. The authorship,
supervision, and certification recorded there refer to the 2018 submission;
this dated revision is a subsequent edition. A separate four-page note,
\texttt{mao-rank-inequality.pdf}, presents the elementary proof on its own.

\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This report studies partition rank inequalities and congruences for cubic
partition pairs. After introducing partitions, generating functions, and
basic $q$-series identities, we discuss Dyson's rank and the $M_2$-rank of
partitions without repeated odd parts.

The principal addition in this revised edition is an elementary completion
of the approach of Barman and Sachdeva to Mao's rank inequality. Writing
\[
 d(n)=N_2(0,6,n)+N_2(1,6,n)-N_2(2,6,n)-N_2(3,6,n),
\]
we prove
\[
 d(3n+2)\ge p_S(\floor{n/3})\ge1\qquad(n\ge0),
\]
where $p_S(m)$ counts partitions of $m$ into parts not congruent to $2$
modulo $4$. The proof pairs consecutive terms of an alternating Lambert
series and retains the partition-generating product needed for
coefficientwise positivity. It includes the previously untreated
progression $d(9m+8)$.

The second part surveys cubic partition pairs and the congruences
conjectured by Lin, presenting the deductions from the work of Gireesh and
Mahadeva Naika. These results are distinguished from the 2026 addition to
the rank-inequality argument.

\chapter*{Conclusion}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Conclusion}
The elementary argument in Section~\ref{sec:completion} completes the
rank-inequality problem investigated in the original project. It proves
positivity in all three progressions $9m+2$, $9m+5$, and $9m+8$, with the
common lower bound $p_S(m)$. The essential step is to establish positivity
after multiplication by the full partition-generating product; positivity
of the numerator alone is neither assumed nor required.

The proof concerns the progression $3n+2$ and uses only the identities already available in the
2018 report. The cubic partition-pair chapters explain known congruences
and their proofs, with their original attribution retained.

The accompanying scripts provide exact coefficient checks:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{verify\_completion.py} checks the rank-inequality argument.
\item \texttt{verify\_identities.py} checks the product identities and congruences.
\end{itemize}
These checks verify formulas and indexing over finite ranges; the proofs
and cited theorems establish the results for all indices.
